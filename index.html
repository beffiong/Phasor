03 — Episodes

The world’s largest event-based dataset.
and getting larger

Episodes is the data layer underneath Phasor. We have over 1000 event-based datasets (and growing) collected and structured for models that learn from change instead of frames.

It’s also a live research surface. Our data and pipeline are used by two frontier research labs and five academic groups. If you’re researching neuromorphic perception, navigation, or sensorimotor control, this is the data you’ve been looking for.

What we build

Phasor builds the world’s first embedded neuro-cognitive robotic foundational model. A novel continual learning architecture, inspired by neuroscience — trained on less data, generalising quicker on unseen spatial reasoning problems, updating its own weights in the field.

Chip-agnostic by design. Runs on whichever brain-inspired silicon wins. BrainChip is our first design partner.

Episodes

Episodes is the data layer underneath Phasor — 652 event-based datasets, collected and structured for models that learn from change instead of frames. It’s how we train NeuroAI.

It’s also a live research surface. Used by one frontier research lab and four academic groups, all training their own models on it. If you’re researching neuromorphic perception, navigation, or sensorimotor control, this is the data you’ve been looking for.
