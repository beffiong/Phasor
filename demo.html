What you're looking at

Two ideas about memory, arguing until they agree.

One remembers by what happened together. The other remembers by when. M7 is built from both, on purpose.

The coral core holds onto things by association, the way a smell can bring back a whole afternoon. The gold ring holds onto things by timing, knowing what's worth keeping, and when it's safe to let go.

Neither is enough alone. What you're watching is the argument between them, being settled, thousands of times a second.

Overall success rate vs. training step (10 tasks × 10 rollouts per checkpoint)

Loss stayed healthy and finite throughout (0.02 to 0.15) while success climbed in real, if noisy, steps. CloseFridgeDrawer alone went 20% → 30% → 70% → 80% across the last four checkpoints, the most convincing monotonic learning signal in the project so far. Training was deliberately stopped at step 86,000 once the checkpoint was healthy enough to warm-start the full run, this was never meant to be the leaderboard number on its own.
