Track 1 — deterministic head, 65 tasks
Atomic-65 scale-up
The first real, non-zero results in the project's history — and the run that produced the warm-start checkpoint everything downstream now builds on.
Complete
Overall success rate vs. training step (10 tasks × 10 rollouts per checkpoint)

Loss stayed healthy and finite throughout (0.02–0.15) while success climbed in real, if noisy, steps — CloseFridgeDrawer alone went 20% → 30% → 70% → 80% across the last four checkpoints, the most convincing monotonic learning signal in the project so far. Training was deliberately stopped at step 86,000 once the checkpoint was healthy enough to warm-start the full run — this was never meant to be the leaderboard number on its own.
